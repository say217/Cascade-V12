    Priority & Action

    Detection is the first stage, not the final output.

    Every frame moves through four connected stages before it reaches a human operator, converting a single classification into a coordinated recommendation.

    01

    Visual Detection

    Frame-level inference through specialised flood and landslide models isolates hazard regions and assigns confidence scores.

    02

    Spatial Intelligence

    Detection coordinates are reprojected onto map data, anchoring each event to real terrain, waterways, and settlements.

    03

    Risk Assessment

    Contextual signals such as weather trajectory and terrain slope are fused with detection confidence to produce a severity index.

    04

    Decision Support

    Prioritised zones and recommended actions surface in a lightweight command interface built for simultaneous multi-feed monitoring.

    Built For Terrain At Risk

    Engineered for geographically vulnerable regions.

    Floods and landslides generate conditions that shift by the hour, particularly across mountainous and riverine geography such as North Bengal. Response teams are routinely asked to interpret drone footage, satellite imagery, weather telemetry, and field observation simultaneously, a manual burden that delays the identification of the zones that matter most.

    C.A.S.C.A.D.E. reduces that bottleneck by fusing every signal into a single, continuously updated operating picture.

    • Drone and satellite feed ingestion, processed in near real time.
    • Weather and hydrological context layered against detection confidence.
    • Map-anchored geospatial output for direct field coordination.
